# Townsend household assistant — Privacy

- **What is collected:** demographics, signed medication orders, lab observations and diagnostic reports for household members who gave consent; insurance claims and statements the household uploads itself.
- **Where it lives:** on a single home server on the household's private network. Nothing is hosted with a third party.
- **Who can see it:** signed-in household members only. Data is never sold, shared, or sent to advertisers or data brokers.
- **Automated processing:** an AI assistant reads the data to answer the household's own questions and flag bills that do not match insurance statements. Excerpts may be sent to the AI provider (Anthropic) under its API terms, which do not train on that data.
- **Retention:** kept while the household uses the tool; deleted on a member's request or when consent is revoked.
- **Security:** encrypted at rest on the home server; sign-in required; no public internet exposure.
