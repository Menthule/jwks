# Townsend household assistant — Terms

This application is a private, self-hosted tool used only by members of one household. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the family that runs it.

- It reads health information (demographics, medications, lab results, diagnostic reports) from a member's own patient portal only after that member signs in and gives consent.
- It is read-only: it never schedules, cancels, messages, orders, pays, or writes anything back to any provider or insurer.
- A member may revoke access at any time from their patient portal's third-party-apps settings; the copied data is then deleted from the household server on request.
- The software is provided as-is for personal use, with no warranty.

Contact: the household administrator (Gary Townsend), via the email on file with the patient portal.
